## Summary

Process-wide voice recording and TTS API for the TUI gateway, wrapping `tools.voice_mode` and `tools.tts_tool`. Supports push-to-talk and continuous VAD modes, with a TTS-vs-STT feedback guard to prevent audio loops.

## Details

- **Dual recording modes**: Push-to-talk (`start_recording`/`stop_and_transcribe`) and continuous VAD (`start_continuous`/`stop_continuous`) with auto-restart and silence limits.
- **Key normalization**: `normalize_voice_record_key_for_prompt_toolkit` maps TUI/CLI modifier aliases to `c-x`/`a-x` format, blocking reserved chords and TUI-only modifiers.
- **TTS feedback guard**: `_tts_playing` Event prevents the microphone from capturing the agent's spoken reply, avoiding infinite transcription loops.
- **Debug breadcrumbs**: `_debug` prints directly to `sys.stderr` when `HERMES_VOICE_DEBUG=1`, bypassing standard logging for real-time TUI activity lines.
- **Lazy dependencies**: Audio packages (`sounddevice`, `faster-whisper`) are imported at call time to avoid startup crashes on systems without audio hardware.
