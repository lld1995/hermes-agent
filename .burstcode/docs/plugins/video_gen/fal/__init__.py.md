## Summary

FAL.ai video generation backend plugin. Routes requests to model families (LTX, Pixverse, Veo, etc.) for text-to-video or image-to-video. Handles payload construction, capability gating, and lazy `fal_client` loading.

## Details

- Implements `FALVideoGenProvider` adhering to the `VideoGenProvider` interface.
- Maintains `FAL_FAMILIES` catalog mapping model IDs to endpoints, capabilities, and pricing tiers.
- `_resolve_family()` selects the active model via tool args, env vars, or config fallbacks.
- `_build_payload()` constructs family-specific requests, dropping unsupported keys and clamping durations.
- Lazy-loads `fal_client` and routes to text-to-video or image-to-video endpoints based on `image_url` presence.
